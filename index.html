AptAdmin – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

AptAdmin (Apartment Admin) helps apartment committees track maintenance dues, payments and expenses.

Data stays on your device

Flats, residents' names and phone numbers, payments and expenses are stored only on your phone. We do not operate a server and cannot see your data. We do not sell or share it.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose "Export to Google Drive", you sign in with your Google account and a backup file is saved in an "AptAdmin Backups" folder in your own Drive. The app requests only the drive.file permission: it can access files it created itself and nothing else in your Drive. You can delete the backups from Drive at any time. Restoring from Drive downloads the backup you pick to your device.

Subscription and payments

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play, the Apple App Store, or (on the website) Stripe. We use RevenueCat to verify whether a subscription is active; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID (or, on the website, the e-mail you enter) and purchase status. We never see or store your card details.

WhatsApp

Reminder buttons open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. You send the message yourself; the app does not send anything on its own.

Photos and files

Bill photos you attach to expenses are stored on your device and included in backups you create. The app does not upload them anywhere unless you export a backup to Drive.

App lock and backups

If you turn on the app lock, the app asks your phone to confirm it is you with your fingerprint, face or phone screen lock. This is handled entirely by your phone: the app has no PIN of its own and never receives your biometric data or phone passcode. Backups can be protected with a password you choose: the file is encrypted on your device, and the password is never stored or sent anywhere. If you forget it, the backup cannot be recovered.

Reminders and error reports

The optional weekly backup reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone; no server or push service is involved. If the app hits an error, a short technical note (error message, app version, phone model) is kept on your device only. It is sent only if you tap "Report a problem" and choose an app to send it with. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting service. Google Play and the App Store may show us anonymous crash statistics from devices whose owners have opted in to share them.

Activity log

The app keeps a log of changes (for example "payment recorded", "flat deleted") on your device so your committee can review what changed. It is stored with your data and included in your backups.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → "Erase all data on this device". Uninstalling the app also removes the data. Backup files you saved elsewhere (including in Google Drive) stay until you delete them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
